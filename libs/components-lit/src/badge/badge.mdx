## Styles

### Default / Primary

The primary badge is used to indicate an active status (example: device management page) or provide
additional information (example: type of emergency access granted).

### Subtle

The subtle badge is typically the default badge style. It is often used to indicate neutral
information.

### Success

The success badge is used to indicate a positive status, OR to indicate a feature requires a Premium
subscription.

### Danger

The danger badge is used to indicate a negative status.

### Warning

The warning badge is used to indicate a status waiting on an additional action from the active user.

## Truncation

Text longer than `--bit-badge-max-width` (default ~25ch) is truncated with an ellipsis and the full
text is shown in a tooltip. Set `no-truncate` to disable, or `tooltip` to show different text.

## Accessibility

Follows color WCAG color contrast guidelines for small text.
