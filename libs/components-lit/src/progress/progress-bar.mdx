## Slots

- `slot="label"` — visible label; also the bar's accessible name.
- `slot="start"` — hint under the start of the bar. Defaults to "N% complete"; hide it with
  `hide-start-hint`.
- `slot="end"` — hint under the end of the bar.

## Accessibility

The bar has `role="progressbar"` with `aria-valuenow` from 0 to 100, labelled by the label slot.
Without a label, set `accessible-name`; otherwise it falls back to "Progress bar \{number\}". Use
`aria-value-text` to override the announced value. To reference the bar from outside (e.g.
`aria-describedby`), use its `ariaTarget` with element reflection.
