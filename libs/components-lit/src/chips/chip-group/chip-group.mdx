Every `ChipGroupItem` needs an `id` and a `label`; `variant`, `startIcon` and `accessibleName` are
optional. Activating a chip, in the row or the overflow popover, fires `chipSelect` with the item.
Selecting from the popover closes it first.

## Dismissible

With `dismissible`, chips render as input chips and fire `chipDismiss`; removing them is left to the
consumer. Dismissing from the popover keeps it open until nothing is left in it.

<Canvas of={stories.Dismissible} />

## Overflow

Chips that don't fit are collected behind a `+N` chip (named "Show N more"). The first chip is
pinned and truncates at very small widths. When the focused chip is packed away, focus moves to the
`+N` chip; when that goes away, to the last chip in the row.
