## When to use

The marketing card is a variant of [`<bit-card>`](/docs/component-library-cards-card--docs) that
adds a branded border to draw attention to promotional or upsell content — plan upgrades, feature
announcements, and similar calls to action. Reach for a plain `<bit-card>` for neutral,
presentational content; reserve the marketing card for content that is meant to stand out.

<Canvas of={stories.Default} />

## Backgrounds

Content projected into the `background` slot fills the card behind the main content. The background
layer sits behind the card content and is clipped to the card's rounded corners, so it can be a
solid color, a gradient, an image, or an inline SVG.

### Color or gradient

A simple `div` sized with `tw-size-full` fills the background layer.

<Canvas of={stories.WithBackground} />

### Image

An `<img>` uses `tw-size-full` with `tw-object-cover` so it fills the card and crops to its aspect
ratio instead of stretching. Decorative background images use `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
Ensure the projected content keeps sufficient contrast against the image.

<Canvas of={stories.WithImageBackground} />

### Inline SVG

An inline `<svg>` uses `tw-size-full` with `preserveAspectRatio="xMidYMid slice"` to cover the card.
Color its shapes with theme-token `tw-fill-*` utilities so the artwork tracks the active theme, and
mark it `aria-hidden="true"` since it is decorative.
